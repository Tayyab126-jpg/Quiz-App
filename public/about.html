About Quiz Game

Learn, play, and level up

Quiz Game is a local-first trivia arena with progressive levels, offline-friendly mini-games, and Arena Buddy—an AI teammate that can help you learn and make the most of your game progress.

Built for players

Choose from offline-friendly quiz topics, try the animal Memory Match game, and ask Arena Buddy for help when you have an internet connection. Your profile and game progress stay on this device; they do not sync to other devices.
